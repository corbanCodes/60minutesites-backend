Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

60 Minute Sites ("we," "us") builds and hosts websites for small businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it in plain English on purpose.

The short version: we collect the contact details you give us and basic analytics about how people use our site. We use them to build your website, answer you, and measure our advertising. We don't sell your personal information. Email hello@60minute-sites.com any time to see, correct, or delete what we have.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details — your name, business name, phone number, and email when you fill in a form, book a call, or create an account.
  • Project details — what you tell us about your business, your goals, your timeline, current website, and advertising budget.
  • Account information — if you create a login, your name, email, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in readable form).
  • Content you send us — photos, logos, copy, and other material you want on your website.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage data — pages viewed, links clicked, approximate location by IP address, browser and device type, and the ad or link that brought you here (UTM parameters).
  • Cookies and similar technologies — used to keep you logged in, remember your progress in a form, and measure advertising performance.

2. How we use it

  • To answer you, schedule calls, and build and support your website.
  • To process payments and send invoices and receipts.
  • To send service messages — booking confirmations, account and billing notices, and lead notifications.
  • To measure and improve our advertising, so we spend less showing ads to people who aren't interested.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent spam and fraud.

3. Advertising and analytics

We advertise on Facebook and Instagram, and we use the Meta Pixel and Google tag on this website to see which ads lead to real inquiries.

When you submit a form, we may send Meta a hashed (scrambled, one-way) version of your email address, phone number, and name so Meta can match your inquiry to the ad you clicked. This is called advanced matching. The scrambling happens in your browser before anything is sent — Meta does not receive readable contact details from this process, and we do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

You can limit ad tracking through Facebook Ad Preferences, Google Ad Settings, or your browser's cookie controls.

5. Website chat

Some websites we build include a chat box. Messages you send there, and any contact details you provide, are stored so the business owner can follow up with you. Chat replies may be generated with the help of AI. Please don't send sensitive information — such as payment card numbers, Social Security numbers, or health details — through chat.

6. Text messages and calls

If you give us your cell number, you agree that we may call or text you about your inquiry and your project. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out; that will never affect service you've already paid for.

7. Information belonging to our customers' visitors

When we host a website for a business, that business's customers may submit forms on it. We store that information on our customer's behalf so they can respond to their own leads. We do not use it for our own marketing. If you submitted a form on a website we host and want your information removed, contact that business directly, or email us and we'll pass the request along.

8. How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and customer records for as long as we're working together and afterward as needed for taxes, accounting, and legal requirements — generally up to seven years for financial records. Marketing and analytics data is kept for a shorter period. You can ask us to delete your information at any time; see below.

9. Security

Connections to our sites are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored hashed, and access to customer data is limited to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and to fix problems quickly when we find them.

10. Your choices and rights

  • See it, correct it, or delete it — email us and we'll handle it.
  • Stop marketing messages — click unsubscribe in any marketing email, or reply STOP to a text.
  • Cookie controls — your browser can block or delete cookies; parts of our site may not work as well.

Depending on where you live — for example California, other U.S. states with privacy laws, or the EU/UK — you may have additional rights, including the right to know what we collect, to request deletion, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We honor these requests regardless of where you live. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use our services after a change means you accept the updated policy.
