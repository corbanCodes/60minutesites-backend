How the billing works

OpenAI charges your key per word in and per word out. A website summary is a few thousand words in and about a hundred out, which is why it costs a fraction of a cent. We show you the real number from OpenAI's own usage figures after every row, not an estimate.

What we never do
We never show your key again after you save it, never put it in a log or an error message, and never add a markup. If a job costs $0.14 at OpenAI, it costs you $0.14.
