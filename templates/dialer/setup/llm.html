Getting an OpenAI key

  1. Go to platform.openai.com/api-keys and sign in. This is the developer side of OpenAI, not the ChatGPT app. A ChatGPT Plus subscription does not cover it and does not give you a key.
  2. Add a payment method under Billing and put $10 of credit on it. Without credit the key is created but every call fails.
  3. Press Create new secret key. Name it 60MS HQ Dialer.
  4. Copy the key the moment it appears. OpenAI shows it once and never again. If you lose it, delete it and make another.
  5. Paste it above and press Save and test. We send one tiny request to check it works.

What this key does, and why OpenAI is the better single choice

What it powers

Every call ends with a recording. This key turns that recording into things you can use:

  • A written transcript of who said what.
  • A summary, three or four lines, written into the lead's notes.
  • The answers to the qualification questions in your playbook, pulled out and filed against the lead so you do not have to retype them.
  • A score out of ten for the rep, with three specific reasons. This is the coaching tool.
  • A follow-up task with a due date, created automatically when the call implies one.
  • A flag when someone asked not to be called again, which adds them to your do-not-call list without anyone needing to remember.

Calls still work without it

Skip this step and everything still dials, connects and records. You get audio you have to listen to instead of text you can read. For one rep that is survivable. For three it is not.

Why OpenAI rather than Anthropic

Two different jobs are happening here. One is turning audio into text. The other is reading that text and writing the summary.

Anthropic cannot do the first one. It has no speech-to-text, so if you pick Anthropic you get summaries and scores built from whatever text the AI voice produced, and nothing at all from a human rep's call. OpenAI does both, with one key and one bill. That is the whole reason it is the recommendation.

If you already have an Anthropic account and want to use it for the writing, you can — but you will need an OpenAI key as well for the transcripts, and this page only holds one key.

Keeping the key safe

  • The key is encrypted in our database. It is never shown back to you and never sent to the browser.
  • Set a monthly spend limit in the OpenAI console under Billing. $20 is plenty and it caps your downside.
  • If a key is ever pasted into a public place, delete it in the console immediately and make a new one. Deleting is instant.
