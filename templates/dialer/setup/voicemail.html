What a voicemail drop is and why it changes a rep's day

What happens on the call

The rep dials, hears the greeting, hears the beep, and clicks one button. The recording plays into the voicemail by itself and the rep is already connected to the next number. They never speak.

Over a day that is the difference between 90 dials and 140. Over a week it is a working day.

The rules

  • Say who you are at the start. Not at the end. Most people stop listening at six seconds, and a message from nobody is deleted.
  • One reason only. The voicemail's job is to get the callback, not to sell.
  • Give a callback number, slowly, twice. Digit by digit. Nobody can write down a number read at conversation speed.
  • Under twenty seconds. Watch the timer; it turns orange when you go past.
  • Record it yourself. A real voice with a real room behind it beats a studio take.

Have two or three

One for the first attempt, one for the third, one for a lead who went quiet after a meeting. The third message should sound different from the first; the same thirty-second recording four times is what makes someone block the number.

If recording does not work

Browser recording needs a secure address and permission to use the microphone. If the Record button is switched off, or the browser refuses, record on your phone's voice memo app and upload the file with the picker above. WAV or MP3 both work.

Where it is stored

In this app's own file store, against your account. It is converted to the format phone networks expect when it is saved, so it sounds the same on every call.
