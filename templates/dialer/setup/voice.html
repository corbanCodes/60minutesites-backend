Why the time limits matter more than the voice

The maximum call length is a spending cap

An AI call bills by the minute, at about 9.4 cents all in. Nothing in a phone network guarantees a call ends. Hold music, a phone tree, a voicemail that never beeps, a line left off the hook — all of these keep a call alive and billing.

One stuck call overnight is roughly $56. Ten of them is a bad week. The cap is the only thing standing between you and that, so set it to a length a real conversation would never exceed. Ten minutes is generous for a cold call; twenty is generous for a booked demo.

Silence cut-off

Separate from the cap. If the line goes quiet for twenty seconds the other person has almost certainly put the phone down without hanging up. Ending there saves the rest of the call.

What a transfer actually does

The AI stays on the line, dials your rep, tells them in one sentence who is waiting and why, then joins the two of you and leaves. The lead, the notes and the recording stay on one record.

If you send transfers to a single number, pick one that is answered. A transfer into voicemail wastes the best call of the day.

Background noise, honestly

Faint room noise makes a synthetic voice easier to listen to, in the same way a quiet office behind a real person does. It is not a disguise and it should never be used as one. Keep the disclosure on.
