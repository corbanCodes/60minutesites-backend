What the three lanes actually cost and what is gated
LanePer minuteWhat it needsGate
Reps dial about 1.4¢ Twilio, one phone number, a playbook None beyond normal calling hours and do-not-call lists.
AI answers about 1.4¢ + 8¢ Twilio, a number, ElevenLabs, an AI agent The AI has to say it is an AI. That is on by default.
AI dials out about 1.4¢ + 8¢ All of the above, plus consent records Blocked to cell phones without consent on file. Some states are blocked entirely.

The phone minutes are billed by Twilio on your own card. The AI voice minutes are billed by ElevenLabs on your own card. Nothing runs through us and nothing is marked up.

Most people start with reps dialing, get the script right on real calls, and only then point an AI at it. A script that does not work when a person reads it will not work when a machine reads it.
