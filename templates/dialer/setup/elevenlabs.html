The first thing we do with it is create the webhook that sends call results back here. A key from a member-level user cannot create a webhook, so the connection succeeds and then every AI call finishes with no transcript and no summary. If you are not the owner of the ElevenLabs workspace, ask whoever is to create the key.

The plan buys how many calls can run at once, not a cheaper rate. Every tier is eight cents a minute. If you only ever run two or three AI calls at a time, the cheap plan costs you exactly the same per minute as the expensive one.

A batch campaign only uses about half your concurrency, so Pro is the realistic floor for a team. The other half is held back for inbound calls and for retries, so a plan that says 20 gives you roughly 10 working lines.

Ten lines, each on a four-minute call, is about 150 conversations an hour and about $48 an hour in voice cost. Price the campaign before you start it.

Skip this step unless you want the AI to talk to people

ElevenLabs is the voice. It is only needed for the two AI lanes: the AI answering your incoming calls, and the AI dialing out. If your reps are making the calls themselves, skip this step entirely and nothing is lost.

What you pay, and to whom

  • ElevenLabs: 8 cents a minute of AI conversation, plus the monthly plan fee.
  • Twilio: about 1.4 cents a minute for the phone line underneath, as always.

So an AI call costs roughly 9.4 cents a minute all in. A four-minute conversation is about 38 cents.

Turn burst pricing off

ElevenLabs offers to let you exceed your concurrency limit rather than queueing the call. It does not warn you when it happens and it bills those minutes at double, 16 cents. We keep it off. Leave it off until you have watched a month of real usage.

Two things that will break the key

  • Pasting it into a public repository. GitHub scans for ElevenLabs keys and ElevenLabs disables them automatically within minutes. The key is encrypted here and never leaves this server.
  • IP allowlisting. ElevenLabs lets you restrict a key to certain addresses. Do not. This app calls out from whichever server address the host happens to give it that day, and an allowlist will break calling at an unpredictable moment.

Picking a plan

Start on Creator at $22 a month while you test. Move to Pro before you run your first real batch, because a 10-line plan gives you about 5 working lines and that is slower than one rep. If you are only using the AI to answer incoming calls, Creator is often enough forever — incoming calls rarely arrive five at a time.
