Two calls at once sounds survivable until you watch it. One rep in a power dialer will use both lines, and a second rep waits. The spam labelling matters more: once a number is flagged, answer rates fall by half and the only fix runs through Voice Integrity.

What Twilio will ask you for

  1. Your legal business name, address and business type, exactly as they appear on your registration.
  2. Your EIN, or your business registration number.
  3. A website or a business email at your own domain.
  4. An authorized representative: a real person's name, job title, email and phone number.
  5. That person's government photo ID and a selfie taken on the spot. Twilio verifies the face against the ID. Have your driver's licence or passport in hand before you start.

Start this now, not later. Twilio quotes up to 48 hours for review, and a rejected submission restarts the clock. Everything else in this setup can go on while you wait — you can connect numbers, write your script and make test calls with the profile still pending.

Why phone companies ask for this at all

Every US carrier now scores incoming calls and decides whether to label them. The score is built from who the carrier believes placed the call. If nobody has proved who you are, your calls are unsigned, and unsigned calls get labelled.

A business profile is how Twilio proves it to the carriers on your behalf. Once it is approved, Twilio signs every call you place at the highest level, which is what the carriers call attestation A.

Timeline

  • Submitting: 10 to 15 minutes if you have the ID and the EIN ready.
  • Review: Twilio says up to 48 hours. In practice most clear the same business day.
  • If it is rejected: Twilio emails the reason. The usual cause is a business name that does not match the registration character for character, or an address that is a mailbox rather than a street address.

After approval

Two more things become available, both free, both worth doing on the numbers step:

  • Voice Integrity — registers your numbers with the analytics companies the carriers use. This is what you use to get a “Spam Likely” label removed. It takes another 24 to 48 hours.
  • CNAM — the caller name, up to 15 characters, that shows under your number on phones that display it.

If Twilio support asks what you are using the numbers for, answer plainly: outbound sales calls to business contacts, from a script, during business hours, with a do-not-call list. Vague answers are what get applications held.
