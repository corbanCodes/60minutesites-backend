Both values are encrypted before they are stored. After this page they are never shown again, only the last four characters of the SID.

Where to find those two values

  1. Open console.twilio.com and sign in. If you do not have an account yet, create one first. It is free to open and you pay only for what you use.
  2. You land on the Account Dashboard. Look for the panel headed Account Info, near the bottom of the page.
  3. Account SID is the first line. Click the copy icon next to it, then paste it above.
  4. Auth Token is below it, hidden behind a Show link. Click Show, copy the value, paste it above.
  5. Press Connect and test. We call Twilio right away. If the call works, this step turns green and shows your balance. If it does not, the exact message Twilio sent back appears at the top of this page.

Make sure you are on the main account, not a subaccount. If there is a dropdown at the top of the console with more than one account in it, pick the top one.

A brand-new Twilio account is a trial account.

A trial can only call phone numbers you have verified one at a time in the Twilio console, and every call starts with a Twilio recording. That makes it useless for real dialing. Add $20 of credit in the console (Billing, then Add funds) and the restriction lifts immediately.

What Twilio is, what it costs, and why the account is yours

Twilio is the phone company underneath this app. It owns the actual phone lines. When a rep clicks Call, this app asks Twilio to place the call.

The account is in your name and the card on it is yours. That means your phone numbers stay yours if you ever leave, your call recordings are in your own account, and we cannot mark up a minute even if we wanted to.

What you will pay Twilio

ItemPrice
Outbound call, US landline or mobileabout 1.4¢ a minute

A rep making 100 dials a day, with 12 minutes of real talk time, costs roughly 20 cents a day in Twilio minutes.

What we create inside your Twilio account

As soon as the test passes, we create two things for you so the browser phone works:

  • An API Key named 60MS HQ Dialer. We use this instead of your Auth Token for day-to-day calling, so you can revoke our access without changing the password to your whole account.
  • A TwiML App named 60MS HQ Dialer. This is the address Twilio sends a call to when a rep picks up in the browser.

Both appear in your console. Deleting them breaks calling until you reconnect here.

If the test keeps failing

  • Authenticate or 20003 means the token is wrong. Click Show in the console and copy it again, with no leading or trailing space.
  • Account not found or 20404 usually means the SID is from a different account than the token.
  • If you recently clicked Rotate on the Auth Token page, only the newest token works.
