Practice mode, and how to read the result

What practice mode is

A switch that makes the system talk to a stand-in instead of to Twilio, ElevenLabs and OpenAI. Every other line of code runs exactly as it does in production: the same rules are checked, the same records are written, the same screens are drawn. The only difference is that no phone rings and no vendor charges you.

It exists so you can click through the whole product, train a new rep, or show someone what a call record looks like, without spending money or calling a real person by accident.

It is switched on either by the server (while this is still being built) or per account. The banner at the top of this page always tells you which state you are in — check it before you press the button.

If the real call does not arrive

  • Nothing rings at all. Usually a trial Twilio account calling an unverified number. The call record will say so.
  • It rings and then drops. The phone number is not pointed at this app. Re-import it on step 4.
  • It connects but there is silence. Check the voice settings on step 8, and that ElevenLabs is connected if you picked an AI agent.
  • The call worked but there is no transcript. The AI key on step 5 is missing or out of credit. The recording is still there.

Reading the call record

Every call gets one page: who was called, what the system observed, the recording, the transcript, the summary, the score out of ten with three reasons, and a timeline of every event the phone network sent. When something goes wrong on a real call later, that timeline is where the answer is.
